MINISTRY OF ENVIRONMENT FOREST AND CLIMATE CHANGE
NOTIFICATION
New Delhi, the 13th January, 2026
G.S.R. 25(E).-- WHEREAS, the Central Government notified the Carbon Credit Trading Scheme,
2023 (hereinafter referred to as the said scheme), vide S.O. 2825 (E), dated the 28th June 2023, in exercise
of the powers conferred by clause (w) of section 14 of the Energy Conservation Act, 2001 (52 of 2001).
AND WHEREAS, under the compliance of said scheme, the Central Government issued the
Greenhouse Gases Emission Intensity Target Rules, 2025 vide notification number G.S.R.739(E), dated the
8th October, 2025 for setting greenhouse gases emission intensity targets of four sectors i.e. Aluminium,
Cement, Chlor-alkali and Pulp and paper;
AND WHEREAS, under the compliance of said scheme, the greenhouse gases emission intensity
targets of four more sectors i.e., Aluminium (Second Aluminium), Petroleum Refinery, Petrochemical and
Textile are to be notified by the Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change;
AND WHEREAS, for this purpose, the draft of the Greenhouse Gases Emission Intensity Target
Amendment Rules, 2025, was published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section
(i), vide notification number G.S.R. 234(E), dated the 23rd June 2025, for inviting objections and suggestions
from all persons;
AND WHEREAS, all the objections and suggestions have been duly received and considered by the
Central Government.
